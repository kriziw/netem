What branding changes

One brand per appliance sets the product name, subtitle, logo, favicon, font and colour palette. It applies to the operator interface, these help pages, printed session reports and the showroom. Project credits and technical NetEm references stay in place.

Choose and edit brands in the app

Settings → Branding lists the brands installed on this appliance and the default look. Use switches brand at once: open pages, reports and the showroom follow on their next refresh, with no restart.

New brand, or Edit on an existing one, opens the editor:

  • name, subtitle and the short mark shown when there is no logo;
  • a logo (SVG, PNG, JPEG or WebP) and a favicon (ICO, PNG or SVG);
  • a font family name with regular and bold WOFF or WOFF2 files;
  • every colour token.

Uploads are limited to 2 MB each and are checked to be what they claim. SVGs must be plain pictures, without scripts, event handlers or external links.

Brands made here are stored privately on the appliance in runtime/brands, or in NETEM_BRANDS_DIR when that is set. They are never part of the public repository. A pack installed the original way, below, appears as installed on the appliance. It is read-only in the editor; Copy and edit makes an editable copy with its logo and fonts.

SD-WAN vendor

Next to the MSP brand, the showroom header and the Command Center show the SD-WAN vendor under test. NetEm takes it from the appliance selected in the Traffic Simulator. Settings → Appliance under test can name the vendor and model instead. The official logos of Fortinet, VeloCloud, Cisco, HPE Aruba, Palo Alto Networks, Versa, Juniper, Check Point, Sophos, Forcepoint, Barracuda, Peplink, Huawei, Ekinops, Cato Networks, SonicWall and WatchGuard are built into NetEm and shown on a white card. A vendor without a logo is shown by name. The logos are trademarks of their owners and only name the appliance under test.

Install a brand pack

A brand pack is a folder with a branding.json manifest and an assets/ folder. Keep it outside the Git checkout, for example in /etc/netem/branding/, so updates never touch it.

branding/
  branding.json
  assets/
    logo.svg
    favicon.png
    regular.woff2
    bold.woff2

Point the service at it with sudo systemctl edit netem:

[Service]
Environment=NETEM_BRANDING_DIR=/etc/netem/branding

Then run sudo systemctl daemon-reload and sudo systemctl restart netem. The service user needs read access to the folder. NetEm notices changes to the pack's files while it runs. Remove the setting to return to the default appearance.

For local development, runtime/branding/branding.json is picked up when no environment setting is present. An explicit setting always wins, even when its pack is invalid.

Manifest

{
  "name": "Example Resilience Lab",
  "subtitle": "Network validation platform",
  "mark": "E",
  "logo": "logo.svg",
  "favicon": "favicon.png",
  "font_family": "Example Sans",
  "fonts": [{"file": "regular.woff2", "weight": 400}, {"file": "bold.woff2", "weight": 700}],
  "tokens": {"accent": "#4050D0", "bg": "#08101C", "surface": "#0D1726", "text": "#EDF4FB"}
}

Every field is optional; the text mark and the system font are the fallbacks. Asset names are relative to assets/. Images can be SVG, PNG, JPEG, WebP or ICO and fonts WOFF or WOFF2. Nothing is loaded from external sites.

Colour tokens

Colours are six-digit hex values:

  • Backgrounds: bg, bg-deep, surface, surface-2, surface-3, surface-hover
  • Borders: border, border-strong
  • Text: text, text-soft, muted, muted-2
  • Brand: accent, accent-strong, blue
  • Status: warning, danger, success

Translucent variants and component colours are derived from these. Set the full palette for a consistent look, keep the status colours easy to tell apart, and check contrast, including white text on primary buttons and the showroom's health colours.

Privacy

Only the generated theme stylesheet and the assets listed in the manifest are served; the manifest itself is not downloadable. Anyone using the appliance or watching the showroom sees the brand, so branding does not keep it secret. Keep brand packs out of public repositories, release bundles, CI artifacts and screenshots.

The full reference is docs/BRANDING.md in the repository.
