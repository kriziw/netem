WAN order sets preference: WAN 1, then WAN 2, then WAN 3. SLA thresholds: latency 150 ms, jitter 30 ms, loss 2%. Independent physical/VM ports and static IPv4 addressing are supported. Switch members, VRFs, secondary addresses and central NAT need manual preparation.
