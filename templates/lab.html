Path MTU testing
Temporarily constrain the WAN bridge and member interfaces to reproduce MTU-sensitive application failures. This is an experimental path-MTU tool, not yet a selective PMTUD-blackhole implementation.
A future phase can add nftables-based oversized-packet drops with ICMP suppression for a true PMTUD blackhole.

Advanced packet impairments
Packet reordering, duplication, corruption and correlated loss are available under Advanced packet impairments on each Dashboard WAN card.
They remain manual overrides and therefore switch that WAN to Custom mode.
