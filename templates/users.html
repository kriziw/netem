Authentik · OpenID Connect

Example: {"netem-admins":"admin", "netem-operators":"operator", "netem-presenters":"presenter", "netem-viewers":"viewer"}. Highest mapped role wins. Unmapped users cannot sign in.

Use a confidential OAuth2/OpenID provider, strict redirect URI matching, a signing key, scopes openid/profile/email, and a groups claim in the ID token. Keep one local administrator for recovery. Existing SSO logins are revoked when these settings change.
